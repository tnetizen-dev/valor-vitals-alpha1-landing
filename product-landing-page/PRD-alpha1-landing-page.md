# PRD: Valor Vitals "Alpha 1" High-Conversion Landing Page

## 1. Core Visual Identity (Inspired by Jocko Fuel)

- **Palette:** High-contrast tactical monochrome (Slate/Charcoal/White) with a single "Safety" accent color (Safety Orange or Zinc Yellow).
- **Typography:** Bold, heavy sans-serif headers to convey discipline and authority.
- **Photography:** "Field-Tested." Authentic, gritty, unpolished imagery of men in "hard use" scenarios (parenting in the rain, first responders on shift, veterans training).

---

## 2. Functional Architecture (Inspired by Hydrant)

### Section 1: The Tactical Buy Box & Social Proof

- **Quantified Specs:** Top of the buy box features a high-contrast bar: **600mg Lion's Mane | 22 Essential Nutrients | 0 Fillers**.
- **Subscription by Default:** Pre-select "Subscribe & Save (10%)". Frame it as the **"Maintenance Protocol"** with benefits listed directly on the toggle: **10% Savings, Free Shipping, 30-Day Readiness Guarantee**.
- **Hard Numbers (Social Proof):** Immediately below the Buy Box, use Hydrant-style specificity: **[Number] Customers | [Number] Capsules Shipped**. Avoid generic superlatives; use real data to build credibility plainly.

### Section 2: The Readiness Equation

- **Logic:** Mirrors Jocko's "Muscle Equation" but uses Callon's "Equipment Maintenance" language.
- **Formula:** **[Fuel: Micronutrients] + [Focus: Lion's Mane] + [Maintenance: Curcumin] = Total Capability**

### Section 3: ICP "Field Reports" (Testimonials)

- **Function:** Instead of generic quotes, use a 4-card grid representing specific ICPs (The Operator, The Protector, The Professional, The Father).
- **Content:** Each card features a "hard-use" lifestyle photo with a 2–3 word benefit label (e.g., **"Clear the Fog"** or **"Stay Asset-Ready"**).
- **Persona Mapping:**
  - **Marcus (The Operator):** Focus on recovery from years of hard use.
  - **Ryan (The Protector):** Focus on energy consistency for his family.

### Section 4: Quantified Ingredient Cards

- **Design:** Jocko-style "Data Sheets" (dark mode cards).
- **Content:** Every ingredient gets a dedicated card with exact dosages (e.g., **600mg Lion's Mane**) and a plain-English explanation of why it is in the "gear list."
  - *Example:* "Curcumin: Used to manage the natural inflammation that comes from hard training and years of service."

### Section 5: The "Body as Gear" Analogy

- **Headline:** The only piece of equipment you carry 24/7.
- **Body Copy:** "Most people run their gear dry and wonder why it fails. Your body is no different. Alpha 1 is the daily maintenance protocol required to stay ready for when life taps you on the shoulder."

---

## 3. Brand Voice Constraints (The "Callon" Rules)

- **Zero exclamation points:** All impact must come from the weight of the data and the honesty of the story.
- **No "Hype" words:** Avoid "Supercharge," "Dominate," or "Biohack."
- **Directness:** Speak like a peer (Marine infantry officer/EMT) to a peer, not a salesman to a lead.

---

## 4. Technical & Conversion Requirements

- **Mobile-First "Buy Box":** Ensure the quantified specs and subscription default are visible without scrolling on standard mobile devices.
- **Collapsible Technicals:** Use collapsible sections for "Detailed Supplement Facts" and "Usage Instructions" to keep the UI rugged and focused.
- **Primary CTA:** "Start Your Protocol" or "Build Your Foundation."
